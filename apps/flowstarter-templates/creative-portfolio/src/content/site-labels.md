  faq:
    title: "The questions we usually get"
    items:
      - question: "What kind of projects do you take on?"
        answer: "Identity, art direction, editorial, and digital work — usually for founders, cultural institutions, and small teams who care about the details. We are happiest on work where craft actually matters to the outcome."
      - question: "How big is the studio?"
        answer: "Small, and that is the point. You work directly with the people designing the work. We bring in trusted collaborators for photography, motion, or development when a project calls for it."
      - question: "Do you only design, or do you build too?"
        answer: "Both. We design identities and the systems around them, and we build the sites that carry them. Design and implementation stay in the same conversation, so nothing gets lost in translation."
      - question: "How do projects usually run?"
        answer: "In tight loops with you involved throughout. We agree on the idea early, work in focused rounds, and keep feedback direct. Fewer, sharper reviews beat a long chain of small ones."
      - question: "Can you work with our in-house team?"
        answer: "Often, yes. We can lead a project end to end or act as an extra set of hands and eyes for a team that already has momentum but needs craft or capacity."
      - question: "What do you need to get started?"
        answer: "A short sense of the project, who it is for, and what a good outcome looks like. From there we will tell you honestly whether we are the right studio for it."
