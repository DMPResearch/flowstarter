      - question: "How is your work different from a large strategy firm?"
        answer: "We are senior-led and small by design. The people who scope the work do the work, we operate from your real data, and we stay through the first cycles of execution rather than handing over at the deck."

        answer: "Fixed scope and fixed fee wherever the question is clear enough to define one. For open-ended diagnostics we use a time-boxed phase first, then scope the next step with you."
      - question: "What do you need from us to start?"
        answer: "A clear view of the decision or problem, access to the people who own it, and the operating data behind it. From a short framing call we can usually propose the right first step."
