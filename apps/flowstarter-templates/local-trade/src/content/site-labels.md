      - question: "How long is the wait for a start date?"
        answer: "It depends on the time of year and the size of the job, but we are usually booking a few weeks ahead. We will give you a realistic date up front rather than an optimistic one we cannot keep."
      - question: "Do you only work in solid wood?"
        answer: "Mostly, yes — solid hardwood and quality engineered timber. We will always explain the options and what makes sense for the job and the budget, rather than upselling you the most expensive thing."
      - question: "Will my house be full of dust and tools for weeks?"
        answer: "No. The bulk of the cutting and finishing happens in the workshop. On site we protect floors, keep tools tidy and clean up at the end of every day."
      - question: "Is your work guaranteed?"
        answer: "Yes. Workmanship is covered by a two-year guarantee, and we stand behind anything we have made. If something is not right, we come back and put it right."
